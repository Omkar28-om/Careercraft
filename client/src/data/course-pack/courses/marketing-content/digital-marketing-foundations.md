# Digital Marketing Foundations

**Domain:** Marketing & Content  
**Level:** Beginner

An original introduction to audience research, SEO, social media, and campaign measurement.

## Module 1: Audience and Strategy

### Define the audience

Describe the audience's needs, context, problems, and preferred channels. Build a clear value proposition instead of trying to appeal to everyone.

### Set measurable objectives

Use specific goals and a time period. Choose metrics that connect to outcomes, such as qualified leads, conversions, retention, or revenue.

## Module 2: Content and Search

### Content planning

Create useful content for a defined audience. Match format and detail to the audience's intent and maintain a consistent publishing workflow.

### SEO basics

Use descriptive page titles, helpful headings, accessible content, descriptive links, and fast mobile-friendly pages. Avoid keyword stuffing and misleading claims.

## Module 3: Campaign Measurement

### Social media planning

Select channels based on audience behaviour and the content format. Use a calendar, clear calls to action, and a consistent brand voice.

### Analytics and experiments

Track relevant metrics, define a baseline, and test one meaningful change at a time when practical. Do not interpret likes alone as business success.
