# Content Creation and SEO

**Domain:** Marketing & Content  
**Level:** Beginner

Plan accessible content, structure pages, research search intent, and measure performance.

## Module 1: Content Briefs

### Write a useful brief

A brief states the audience, problem, goal, key message, format, evidence required, call to action, and success metric.

### Editorial workflow

Plan research, drafting, fact-checking, accessibility review, approval, publishing, and later updates.

## Module 2: On-page SEO

### Search intent

Search intent may be informational, navigational, commercial, or transactional. Create a page that genuinely satisfies the likely need.

### Page structure

Use a clear title, descriptive headings, meaningful link text, alt text for informative images, and concise metadata. Avoid promising results the page cannot deliver.

## Module 3: Improve and Refresh

### Performance measures

Impressions, clicks, click-through rate, conversions, and engagement answer different questions. Interpret them together with the page's purpose.

### Content maintenance

Update outdated facts, broken links, examples, and screenshots. Remove or consolidate pages that are duplicative or no longer useful.
