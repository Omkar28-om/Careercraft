# Healthcare Operations and Analytics Basics

**Domain:** Healthcare & Life Sciences  
**Level:** Beginner

Understand patient-flow concepts, operational metrics, process improvement, and responsible data use.

## Module 1: Healthcare Workflow

### Patient flow

Patient flow describes movement through services such as registration, assessment, treatment, and discharge. Delays may occur when capacity and demand are mismatched.

### Process mapping

A process map makes steps, handoffs, queues, and decision points visible. Teams can use it to locate bottlenecks before proposing changes.

## Module 2: Operational Measures

### Wait time and throughput

Wait time measures delay before a service step. Throughput measures completed cases over a period. Both need clear definitions and time windows.

### Balancing measures

Improving speed should not undermine safety, quality, equity, or staff workload. Evaluate multiple measures rather than optimising one number alone.

## Module 3: Improvement and Ethics

### Small improvement cycles

Define the problem, establish a baseline, test a small change, measure outcomes, and decide whether to adapt, adopt, or abandon the change.

### Ethical data use

Use appropriate permissions, minimise identifiable data, document definitions, and interpret results in context. Operational data should not be treated as a substitute for clinical judgement.
