# Healthcare and Biotechnology Introduction

**Domain:** Healthcare & Life Sciences  
**Level:** Beginner

An introductory overview of cells, biotechnology, evidence, and healthcare data privacy.

## Module 1: Life Science Basics

### Cells and DNA

Cells are fundamental units of life. DNA stores genetic information, and genes are regions of DNA that contribute to biological functions through products such as RNA and proteins.

### Biotechnology

Biotechnology uses organisms, cells, or biological systems to develop products and processes, including diagnostics, medicines, agriculture, and research tools.

## Module 2: Evidence and Quality

### Scientific evidence

A scientific claim should be evaluated using the quality of the evidence, study design, sample, uncertainty, and independent replication where appropriate.

### Quality and traceability

In regulated settings, records, validated procedures, documentation, and traceability help teams investigate errors and maintain consistent processes.

## Module 3: Healthcare Data

### Privacy and confidentiality

Health data can be highly sensitive. Collect only what is necessary, restrict access, use secure storage, and follow applicable privacy and institutional rules.

### Digital health systems

Electronic records and analytics can support workflows, but data quality, access controls, interoperability, and human oversight remain important.
