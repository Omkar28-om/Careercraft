# Business and Project Management Basics

**Domain:** Business & Management  
**Level:** Beginner

Learn objectives, scope, stakeholders, risks, milestones, and team communication.

## Module 1: Project Foundations

### Project goals and scope

A project has a defined objective, boundaries, deliverables, and constraints. Clear scope reduces confusion about what is included and excluded.

### Stakeholders

Identify people affected by or able to influence the project. Clarify their needs, decision rights, communication preferences, and approval responsibilities.

## Module 2: Planning and Delivery

### Milestones and dependencies

Break work into manageable tasks, estimate effort, identify dependencies, and set milestones. Revisit estimates when evidence changes.

### Risk management

Record each meaningful risk, its likelihood and impact, an owner, a mitigation plan, and a trigger for escalation.

## Module 3: Review and Improvement

### Status reporting

A useful status report covers progress, next steps, blockers, decisions needed, and changes to timeline or scope.

### Retrospectives

Review what worked, what did not, and what to change. Assign an owner and due date to each improvement action.
