# Personal Finance and Budgeting Basics

**Domain:** Business & Finance  
**Level:** Beginner

An educational introduction to budgeting, saving, interest, risk, and financial decision-making.

## Module 1: Budgeting

### Income and expenses

Record income and spending over a consistent period. Separate fixed expenses, variable expenses, needs, wants, and irregular costs.

### Emergency savings

An emergency reserve can help cover unexpected essential expenses. A suitable target depends on income stability, obligations, and personal circumstances.

## Module 2: Interest and Debt

### Simple and compound interest

Simple interest is calculated on the original principal. Compound interest earns interest on principal plus previously accumulated interest.

### Borrowing responsibly

Compare the total repayment amount, interest rate, fees, tenure, penalties, and affordability. Understand the terms before signing a loan agreement.

## Module 3: Risk and Decisions

### Diversification

Diversification spreads exposure across assets or holdings, but it does not eliminate all investment risk or guarantee returns.

### Avoiding scams

Be cautious of guaranteed high returns, pressure to act immediately, unclear fees, and requests for OTPs or credentials. Verify providers independently.
